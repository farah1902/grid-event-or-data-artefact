## Main figures

| Number | Source file | Draft caption |
|---|---|---|
| Fig 1 | `fig1_map_coverage` | Data. (a) The UCI 1158 sites: Laâyoune, Foum El Oued and Boujdour share one data logger (15 zones, 10 min, A); Marrakech is a separate 30-min kW system; the UCI Tetouan set (3 zones, 2017) is the transfer test. Coastline: Natural Earth 1:50m; no boundaries drawn. (b) Anomalous states per zone found by rule engine v1 (qualifying runs of holds, straight-line fills, exact zeros and noise floors; missing steps); dotted lines mark clock-change dates. Timestamps as recorded. |
| Fig 2 | `fig2_typology` | Typology. Four data artefacts (A1–A4) and five grid events (G1–G5), each with a measurable signature at a level of the zone–city–region hierarchy and the rules that vote for it (schematic). Straight-line fills, a signature the original plan did not anticipate, are part of A2 and A3. |
| Fig 3 | `fig3_C05_C06_jan2023` | The January 2023 logger episode (C05/C06): simultaneous holds and straight-line fills across all 15 southern zones, then a 15-zone zero fill. Final labels: A2 → A2 → A1. |
| Fig 4 | `fig4_C12_mar2024` | 31 March – 2 April 2024 (C12): L1 and L2 held for 43 h while the other zones are zero-filled. Final labels: A2 → A1 → A2 → G4. |
| Fig 5 | `fig5_clock_2022-10-30` | Clock evidence at 30 October 2022: daily profiles before and after the date and the correlation as a function of the shift δ. δ* = −60 min in the three southern cities (stamped earlier after the date). |
| Fig 6 | `fig6_framework` | Framework. Per-step states and typed items feed 19 labelling rules and a constrained Dawid–Skene label model (weak supervision, no manual labels); a multi-scale residual detector finds anomalies no rule covers; an end model and fusion produce the typed, probabilistic catalogue, which is evaluated by injection (NB4), downstream (NB5) and transfer (NB6). |
| Fig 7 | `fig7_rule_diagnostics` | Rule diagnostics on the real data: coverage per rule and the rule × rule overlap and conflict matrices (rule engine v1). |
| Fig 8 | `fig8_residual_hierarchy` | Level scores (robust z, eq. 15) of the residual hierarchy. During the January 2023 logger zero fill the region part g reaches 218 (forecast residual) while a feeder outage (C02, F4) moves only the zone part v. The nowcast residual of the original plan (eq. 13) behaves the same. |
| Fig 9 | `fig9_detection` | Detection on injected events (30 datasets, test period, mean ± sd over 10 seeds): event F1 (eq. 17) and step-wise AUC-PR of the full method, the rules, the main detector, its ablations, other predictors and the baselines, at 1, 3 and 7 % contamination. |
| Fig 10 | `fig9b_ablations` | Ablations of the main detector: change in event F1 relative to the main detector (mean ± sd of per-seed differences; filled: Wilcoxon p < 0.05). The learned-graph ablation (shaded) leaves F1 equal or slightly higher; the multi-scale bands and the tuned event formation carry the gain. |
| Fig 11 | `fig10_11_attribution` | Attribution on injected events (3 %, test period): (a) confusion of the injected type against the fused label; (b) reliability of the label model q, the end model f and the fusion p. |
| Fig 12 | `fig12_downstream` | Downstream test: LightGBM forecast error (MASE) by cleaning strategy after anomalies, (a) real and (b) injected data, test period 1 Jan – 24 May 2024; (c) outage zone-hours relative to the injected truth for each strategy. |
| Fig 13 | `fig14_transfer_protocol` | Transfer protocol: stage by stage, what is frozen from Morocco, what is re-estimated from Tetouan's unlabelled series, and what a single-city system changes, with the results. Every adaptation is listed in docs/transfer_protocol.md. |
| Fig 14 | `fig13_tetouan` | Transfer to Tetouan without re-tuning: (a) event F1 of every method at 1, 3 and 7 %; (b) attribution confusion of the frozen label model at 3 %. City-wide zero gaps (A1) are read as city outages (G2), the single-city limit. |
